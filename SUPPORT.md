# Support

## Getting help

- **Questions and how-to:** open a GitHub Discussion if they are enabled, or an issue labeled `question`.
- **Bugs:** open an issue with the bug report template. Include your WordPress, PHP and plugin versions, the editor you use (block or Classic), and the steps to reproduce.
- **Feature ideas:** open an issue with the feature request template.
- **Security problems:** do not open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Before you ask

- Check the FAQ in [readme.txt](readme.txt).
- Turn on `WP_DEBUG_LOG` and look for lines starting with `ILSEJ` in `wp-content/debug.log`. Remove API keys and any private content before you paste a log.
- Confirm that your Pinecone index has 768 dimensions and that your Google Cloud service account can call Vertex AI.

## What we cannot help with

Account, billing or quota questions about Google Cloud, Pinecone, Anthropic or OpenAI belong with those providers.

Support is given on a best-effort basis by volunteers and maintainers.
